Frequently
asked questions

What is Base44?

Base44 is a no-code AI platform for building apps, websites and AI agents — no coding required. Describe what you want, and Base44 generates the structure, design and logic for you. It handles the technical side so you can focus on the idea. Beyond building, Base44 gives you access to Superagents — AI agents that automate workflows, manage data and connect to your tools. They work alongside what you build, keeping things running even when you're not.

How does Base44 work?

Base44 uses vibe coding to build apps, websites, agents and more. Describe your idea in plain language — as a rough thought or a detailed spec. Base44 generates the code, structure, design and logic for your app or website. From there, keep iterating by chatting with the AI until everything works the way you want.

How much does it cost?

Base44 has a free plan — no credit card needed to start. The free plan includes monthly credits to build and test your first apps. Paid plans start at $16/month (billed annually) and unlock more credits, advanced features and team capabilities.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Describe what you need in plain language and Base44 handles the rest.

Which AI no-code platform is best to build an app?

The best AI no-code platform is the one that can turn your idea into a fully functional app — not just a static design — without requiring any coding experience. Base44 covers full-stack generation, iteration speed, built-in hosting, integrations and scalability out of the box.

Can I build a complete website with Base44?

Yes. Base44's AI website builder lets you generate a complete, fully functional website in minutes — just describe what you need in plain language. Base44 provides built-in hosting and custom domain support — so publishing takes one click.

What kinds of apps can I build?

Builders use Base44 for internal tools and dashboards, developer productivity apps, customer portals and CRMs, education and training platforms, health and wellness trackers, and a lot more.

What integrations does Base44 support?

Base44 connects to the tools you already use — Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce and more. You can also connect to any external service via API.

How do credits work?

Credits power AI generation. Every time the AI builds, modifies or refines your app, it uses credits. Free plans include a monthly allowance to get you started; paid plans include more.

How are Base44 apps deployed?

Base44 comes with built-in hosting — when your app is ready, it's instantly live and shareable. No deployment process, no setup.

What is a Superagent?

A Superagent is an AI agent that takes action on your behalf. While your Base44 app is the product you're building, your Superagent is the assistant running alongside it — connecting to your inbox, calendar and tools to handle tasks and automate workflows.

Is my data secure?

Yes. User management and authentication are built in, using industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data and your users.
