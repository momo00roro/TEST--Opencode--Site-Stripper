Frequently asked questions

What is Softr?

Softr is a platform for building secure portals and internal tools without a developer. Database, workflow automation, and granular permissions are built in, and it integrates with the tools you already use.

What can you build with Softr?

Client portals, CRMs, inventory systems, intranets, dashboards, ERPs, and knowledge bases. Apps can be internal-only for your team or external-facing for clients, vendors, and partners.

Why use Softr instead of Airtable?

Airtable stores your data; Softr turns it into secure apps with logins, permissions, and workflows. Your clients and teams interact with your data through an interface customized to your business logic and brand.

What systems and data sources does Softr connect to?

Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, HubSpot, monday.com, ClickUp, Supabase, MySQL, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, and AI models from OpenAI and Anthropic for databases and workflows.

Can Softr replace our manual spreadsheets and workflows?

Yes — move beyond spreadsheets into a flexible built-in database that powers your apps and workflows. Connect any tools in your tech stack and automate simple tasks or complex, multi-step processes.

Is Softr secure enough for enterprise IT?

Softr is enterprise-grade with granular permissions, SSO, audit logs, and compliance controls. Invite team members or external clients and partners with confidence in who sees what.

Do you need to know how to code to use Softr?

No. Simply describe what you need and let Softr handle interface, database, and workflows — or build visually yourself. No developer handover is ever required.
