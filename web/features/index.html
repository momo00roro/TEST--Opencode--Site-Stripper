Bounded by design

Useful detail, honest limits.

Hosted analysis is tuned for a lightweight run. Protected, slow, or media-heavy sites may yield partial observations or fewer captures.

  • Up to 10 pagesKey pages selected for one run.
  • 150 secondsHosted analysis wall limit.
