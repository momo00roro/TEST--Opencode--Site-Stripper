Good to know

A few quick answers.

Does Site Stripper use an AI model to analyze a site?+

No. It gathers structured website observations and packages them for design and frontend workflows, including AI-assisted rebuilding.

What can I analyze?+

Public HTTP or HTTPS websites. Site Stripper follows same-origin pages and never submits source-site forms or bypasses bot verification.

Are screenshots always included?+

Capture files are included when a run successfully captures them. Site behavior and hosted limits can make a result partial.

Do I need an account or payment details?+

No. The current alpha opens the analyzer directly. Paid plans are being finalized.
