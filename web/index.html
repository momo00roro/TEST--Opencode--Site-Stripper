Limits and coverage

Know what each run includes.

How many pages can one run analyze?

Hosted runs can analyze up to 10 selected pages in up to 150 seconds.

Are screenshots included in every pack?

Capture files are included when available. Source-site behavior and hosted limits can make captures partial or unavailable.

Does Site Stripper rebuild the website?

No. It gathers reference material to guide a separate design and development workflow.
